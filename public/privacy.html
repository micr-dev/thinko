thinko privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-03-20

thinko is a browser-based Windows XP desktop recreation. It stores only the data needed to run the site, moderate drawing submissions, and publish approved artwork.

What data is collected

  • Drawings and commission images that are submitted through the site
  • Submission titles and moderation metadata
  • Admin authentication session cookies for moderation access

How data is used

  • To review submitted artwork before publication
  • To display approved drawings in My Pictures
  • To authenticate the site administrator for moderation actions

Cookies

thinko uses session cookies only for admin authentication. It does not currently use analytics cookies or advertising cookies.

Data deletion

If you want a submitted drawing or commission removed, email contact@micr.dev with enough detail to identify it.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact contact@micr.dev.
